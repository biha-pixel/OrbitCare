## High-Level Project Summary

OrbitCare helps astronauts observe personal changes, compare measurements with a fixed reference, understand sustained deviations, and record reviews offline. An optional mission introduction and five-stage interactive demo make the real workflow easy to explore. Mission Decision Replay reveals only information available on the selected day. A separate NASA OSD-575 explorer preserves original laboratory observations and provenance. Crew Assist provides approved-procedure signposting and optional local incident handoffs through a labeled fictional drill. Monitoring scenarios are synthetic; actual NASA data remain separate. This research prototype does not diagnose, treat, contact medical support, or validate clinical thresholds.

## NASA Data

NASA research informs the measurement context and the limits of the workflow; it does not validate our engineering thresholds.

- NASA OSDR OSD-575, study version 3; DOI 10.26030/mc5d-p710. We integrated 56 original sodium/potassium observations from four Inspiration4 participants and seven ground sampling visits in a separate research explorer. Original mmol/L values, coded participant/visit labels, methodology, source CSV, checksum and reproducible transformation are preserved. These laboratory observations do not calibrate or validate heart-rate, sleep or fatigue rules. Study: https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-575 ; DOI: https://doi.org/10.26030/mc5d-p710
- NASA Standard Measures informs repeated, contextual human-spaceflight monitoring: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/standard-measures/
- NASA sleep and irregular-schedule risk informs sleep/circadian context: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-from-inadequate-sleep-and-irregular-schedules/
- NASA Human Research Program risks informs physiological adaptation and scope: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/
- NASA cardiovascular adaptations informs heart-rate relevance and interpretation limits in the explorer: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/risk-of-cardiovascular-adaptations/
- NASA Five Hazards informs spaceflight context and explicit monitoring boundaries: https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/
- NASA health and medical-care requirements inform approved-procedure and designated-personnel boundaries: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/3-0-health-and-medical-care/
