## High-level summary

OrbitCare guides astronaut self-monitoring, explains personal changes and preserves review records offline. A body-system explorer connects recorded indicators with NASA research and clear limits. Measurement guides and transparent rules support interpretation; urgent concerns lead separately to approved mission procedures. Synthetic scenarios stay separate from original OSD-575 research. Research prototype, not diagnosis, emergency triage or certified medical care.

## NASA data and resources actually used

- NASA Standard Measures: repeated, contextual human-spaceflight monitoring research.
- NASA sleep and irregular schedules risk: sleep/circadian context and limits.
- NASA HRP risks: physiological adaptation context.
- NASA cardiovascular adaptations and Five Hazards: indicator relevance and scope boundaries in the human health explorer and evidence panels.
- NASA health/medical-care and medical-training requirements: approved-procedure and designated-personnel boundaries in the independent urgent-concern pathway. No treatment protocol or contact system is implemented.
- NASA OSDR OSD-575, DOI 10.26030/mc5d-p710: 56 original sodium/potassium observations from four Inspiration4 participants and seven ground visits. Original mmol/L values, participant/visit labels, methodology, checksum, and reproducible transformation are preserved in a separate research view. These data do not calibrate or validate monitoring thresholds.
