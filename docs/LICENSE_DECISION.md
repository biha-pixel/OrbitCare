# Source-code license decision for owner approval

**Recommendation: retain MIT for original OrbitCare code and documentation.** It is already the repository license, is OSI approved, and preserves continuity with the submitted source. The copyright notice now names **Bihandu Liyanage and Nesandu Liyanage**, consistent with their stated collective authorship. Final release and this licensing choice still require the owners' approval.

The [Space Apps participant terms](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/), retrieved in a rendered browser on November 16, 2026, require original contributions to be freely available or under an OSI-compliant license. They provide Apache 2.0 as NASA's default when a specific compliant license is not chosen. That does not require replacing explicit MIT licensing. [MIT](https://opensource.org/license/mit) and [Apache 2.0](https://opensource.org/license/apache-2-0) are OSI-approved.

| Choice | Fit | Consequences |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MIT — recommended** | Existing short permissive grant, continuity with submitted source | Retain copyright/permission notices. No Apache-style explicit patent grant/termination clause. |
| **Apache-2.0 — alternative** | Permissive terms with explicit patent/contribution provisions | Both rights holders must authorize original-work relicensing. Retain third-party notices, relevant NOTICE files and required modification notices. Earlier MIT copies remain MIT. |

[Apache-2.0.proposed.txt](licenses/Apache-2.0.proposed.txt) is a prepared alternative only; it does **not** activate Apache licensing for OrbitCare. The root [LICENSE](../LICENSE) remains MIT until another choice is approved. No source header or package metadata claims Apache.

Neither choice relicenses NASA data, fonts, music, icons, skills or dependencies. Compatibility concerns preserving their separate terms, not absorbing them into our license. [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identifies actual distributed materials and obligations. This recommendation is not a legal warranty, clinical certification or NASA endorsement.
