## First visit and investigation

The application opens in the existing synthetic Sustained change scenario on day 28. The brief uses the same analysis: heart rate 60 → 71 bpm (+11); sleep 8.0 → 6.2 h (−1.8); fatigue 2 → 4 (+2 categories). Heart rate has met its prototype rule for seven consecutive days. These are fixture results, not medical classifications. The interface identifies the research prototype and synthetic simulation before interaction.

Investigate the change opens the investigation and focuses its timeline heading. The manual path from reference day 14 visits day 15, first change on day 22, persistence on day 23 and review on day 24. The indicator selector coordinates the narrative, original-unit chart and explanation. The selected-day calculation, reference quality, reading origin, practical next step and local review form remain available. All observation tables, complete history export, check-in guides, urgent pathway, methodology, original NASA data and offline tools remain accessible.

The Human Health Explorer opens on the corresponding heart-rate or sleep/fatigue topic, uses the same selected day, and returns to an open investigation. Activity remains contextual, with no independent fitness score. Serious acute concerns remain reachable independently of the trend algorithm.
