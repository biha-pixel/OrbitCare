# OrbitCare judging-readiness review

Subjective engineering/product assessment, not official scores, award probability, or a winning guarantee. Current 2026 award definitions/eligibility were not independently retrieved. The five criteria below follow the participant-supplied plan; historical category positioning must not be presented as verified current guidance.

| Criterion | Evidence in the working application | Remaining risk / improvement |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | Complete record→personal comparison→explanation→local review workflow; genuine offline reopening | No astronaut usability study or operational evaluation; indicators cover only a small health subset |
| Creativity | Integrated personal context, visible reasoning, data gaps, mission replay, and offline review | Components are familiar; the distinctive contribution is integration and participant-directed workflow, not clinical novelty |
| Validity | Reproducible rules, missingness handling, original units, tests, checksum-verified NASA subset, separate evidence/assumptions | Rules, fatigue scale, thresholds and clinical performance remain unvalidated; small short-flight cohort cannot establish long-duration behavior |
| Relevance | Gather indicators, evaluate changes within explicit limits, record review intent | Review is a local record and cannot send support requests; no live sensors or full mission procedures |
| Presentation | Unified change brief, reference-to-reading explanation, marked mission progression, five-size visual review, 28.000-second 1080p captioned demonstration, seven native 16:9 slides | Participant must review final media/fields, publish source manually if used, and confirm official submission |

Best Use of Science/Data/Technology and Best Mission Concept are historical positioning references, not verified 2026 eligibility. A separate real-data view strengthens provenance and scientific comprehension; it does not establish clinical validity. The strongest claims are inspectable reasoning, reliable offline software behavior, and honest data separation.

The upgrade increases the visibility of existing capabilities. It does not introduce validated thresholds, clinical risk scores, diagnosis, live astronaut telemetry, real Earth communications, or evidence of superior medical outcomes. Those would be unsupported claims. NASA OSD-575 remains descriptive research, not a calibration or validation cohort for the monitoring rules. Offline readiness follows preparation; the first-ever unprepared offline visit still cannot work.

The subsequent guided-care upgrade adds measurement methods, status-specific next steps, a separate urgent-procedure signpost and a three-region body-to-evidence explorer with purposeful motion. This strengthens Relevance and Presentation and supports a clearer Impact story. The explorer highlights selected topics, not healthy/unhealthy organs. Urgent concern directs to approved procedures without triage, treatment or contact. CHALLENGE_ALIGNMENT.md contains the full requirement/feature/code/test/source/limitation matrix and a five-criterion review. Clinical validity and user-comprehension study limits remain unchanged.
